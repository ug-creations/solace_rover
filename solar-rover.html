Solace Rover Demo uses simulated telemetry

Send the machine first.

Rescue teams often enter hazardous, unmapped or toxic spaces with no warning. Solace Rover scouts ahead and streams back what it finds, so people go in informed.

Onboard: ESP32 board, four IR obstacle sensors at the corners, temperature and humidity sensors, OLED display, WiFi.

How the data travels

1ESP32

C++ firmware

Reads IR, temperature and humidity. Sends raw data over HTTP and WiFi.

2Flask server

Python

Receives the raw telemetry, sorts it and passes it on.

3Gemma

Local AI

Turns raw numbers into a plain-language status message.

4Dashboard

React.js

Live status: moving or stopped, obstacles, temperature, humidity.

5WhatsApp

Emergency only

Instant alert to the team when a critical hazard appears.
